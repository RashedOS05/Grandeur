# Trees

roots are level 0, and it increases with every level

### Full Tree

All **internal** vertices have 2 children

### Balanced Tree

All leaves are at levels h (highest) or h-1

## Tree Traversal

### Preorder:

Root -> left -> right

### Postorder

Left -> right -> root
