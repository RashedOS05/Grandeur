### Reflexive: all pairs are connected to themselves

### Irreflexive: ==none== of the pairs are connected to themselves

A relation can be Reflexive, Irreflexive, or neither (it's not a this or that situation).
Same goes for Symmetric and Antisymmetric relations

### Transitive: If you can go from a to b, and from b to c,  then you should be able to go **directly** from a to c.

### Partial Ordering: Transitive, Reflexive, and Antisymmetric

Ex: **less than or equal to**
