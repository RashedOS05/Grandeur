###### A consumer has initial income of $100 and faces prices of Px = $1 and Py = $5, graph the budget line, and show how it changes when the price of good X increases to Px = $5.

#### First part of the question:

Income = Px . Qx + Py . Qy
100 = 0 + 5 \* Qy
100 = 5 \* Qy
Qy = 20 Units
Qx = 1 \* Qx
Qx = 100 Units

#### Second part:

100 = 5 Qx --> Qx = 100/5 = 20 Units

#### Profit Maximization Rule

MR (Marginal Revenue) = MC (Marginal Cost)

MC = Change in TC / change in Q

MR = Change in TR / Change in Q

#### Calculating The Average

Average Fixed Cost: Fixed Cost / Q

Average Variable Cost: Variable Cost / Q

Average Total Cost: Tc / Q

### Total Cost (Tc)

Fixed Cost: Costs that do not change with quantity
Variable Cost: Costs that change with quantity

If Fixed Cost = 0 --> we're in the long run
otherwise short run
