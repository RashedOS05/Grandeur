## Normative vs. Positive:
__Positive:__ a statement that can be fact-checked (<font color="#ffc000">verifiable</font>)
__Normative:__ an opinion that can't be fact-checked

## Trade-off

To get one thing, you have to give up another due to <font color="#ffc000">scarcity of resources</font>.

**Scarcity:** Economics is based on the assumption of limited resources.

## Opportunity Cost

##### <font color="#ffc000">Highest-valued alternaitve</font>

_Ex: if you pick a business that gives 100k over one that gives 90k and another that gives 80k, the 90k business would be the opportunity cost._

**Opportunity cost cannot be more than one option**
